The response covers these points:

- Notes that a 99-99.6 % (ASHRAE) design temperature would lower the load.
- Warns against oversizing (e.g. units at or above 1.5× the required power, or prefers being on the edge with backup heating elements).
- Recommends system volume of about 15-20 L/kW.
- Says a buffer is probably not needed for a single open underfloor zone, and advises balancing circuits by room heat loss instead of room thermostats.
- Mentions a large DHW coil (about 0.2 m²/kW, minimum 1.5 m²) or primary pipes sized for about 1 m/s.
- Does not invent prices, tariffs, money amounts or subsidy amounts. Percentage savings derived from the 2-3 % per °C rule are fine.
