The response covers these points:

- Identifies the motorised valves closing as cutting flow below the unit's minimum, causing low-flow errors.
- Explains the risk of a frozen or broken plate heat exchanger in cooling when flow is too low.
- Recommends a differential bypass valve set above the unit's minimum flow.
- Recommends adequate system volume (about 15-20 L/kW) or a volumiser.
- Says the "internal pump is too weak" claim is usually not true and to check the pump curve against the system pressure drop.
- Suggests isolating the fault (e.g. running the unit against the volume/buffer alone) or checking refrigerant pipe sizing/charge.
- States that in cooling, low flow can destroy the unit, so flow and volume are critical.
