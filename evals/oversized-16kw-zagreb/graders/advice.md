The response covers these points:

- Explains that the minimum output above the mild-weather load causes on/off cycling.
- Explains that TRVs/room thermostat closing reduce open volume and flow, making cycling worse; recommends keeping zones open and/or adding volume (15-20 L/kW, volumiser or bypass).
- Advises turning the heat pump down rather than closing valves, and balancing the radiators.
- Says 55 °C flow is too high: each +1 °C costs about 2-3 %, aim for about 45 °C or lower.
- Asks for or recommends checking the unit's minimum output, flow rate and/or system volume.
- Does not invent error codes, prices, tariffs or money amounts. Percentage savings derived from the 2-3 % per °C rule are fine.
